The main task was to prepare a strategy for launching a new product to the market by the time 
the unit economy was established. 
In the process of work, the following tasks were completed: 
1) Identify trends, anomalies and atypical patterns in the data.
2) Analyze possible causes of these phenomena.
3) Generate practical recommendations for business based on the insights obtained: possible steps to solve
the identified problems or their further research.
5) Create a detailed analytical report with descriptive statistics of the payment domain.

Data: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1S2WtTa2gWpLoTurYBe0XD21ilUZx6UNl/view

Dataset structure:
1. order_id – unique identifier of the payment order;
2. event_time – date and time of the transaction;
3. user_id – unique user identifier;
4. price – transaction amount in currency;
5. payment_number – transaction type (first or repeated);
6. transaction_status – transaction status (successful or unsuccessful);
7. card_brand – card brand;
8. card_type – card type;
9. bank_name – card issuing bank;
10. error_type – payment error type (details in the link);
11. currency – payment currency;
12. card_country – country code of the issuing bank.
